# طلبات توليد بدائل الأيقونة

استُخدمت أداة توليد الصور المدمجة. الصورة الحالية مرجع للألوان والهوية، وكل بديل ناتج عن طلب مستقل.

## البديل 1

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one square Android app/store icon, designed to remain instantly clear at 32–48 pixels.
Primary request: Redesign the provided icon into a much clearer, distinct alternative for an Arabic daily adhkar and tasbih app called اناء. Input image is a brand palette and identity reference, not a shape that must be preserved.
Subject: A very clear single Arabic letter ن (noon) as a bold, beautifully balanced rounded U-shaped bowl with ONE large circular copper dot directly above its open center. The bowl must read as Arabic noon at a glance: low rounded bowl, short shoulders, thick simple continuous silhouette, no tall flame stroke, no extra dots, no stylized flourishes.
Style/medium: exceptionally clean flat vector-like brand mark, solid geometry, smooth curves, precise and modern.
Color palette: deep forest green #185348 for the bowl, copper #A66E4E for the dot, solid warm ivory #F4F2E8 edge-to-edge background.
Composition: centered single large symbol occupying about 66 percent of width; bold stroke; generous clear breathing space; everything inside the central 70 percent safe area. Full square canvas, straight corners.
Text: no words, no app name, no captions, no English, only the single noon monogram.
Constraints: produce ONE standalone icon only. No mockup, grid, phone, border, bevel, gradients, textures, drop shadow, outer rounded rectangle, glow, watermark, or small decoration. Readable symbol over ornamental complexity.
```

## البديل 2

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one square Android app/store icon, optimized for instant recognizability at 32–48 pixels.
Primary request: Create a second distinctly different clear icon proposal for an Arabic daily adhkar and tasbih app called اناء. Input image is only a brand color palette reference.
Subject: A simple unmistakable loop of prayer beads (tasbih): nine LARGE perfectly round ivory beads arranged in a compact near-circular loop, separated by clean generous green gaps, connected visually to one small copper separator at bottom-right and a SHORT simplified ivory tassel made from a single solid shape. No thin chain or minute details. Ring and tassel together must instantly read as prayer beads, not gears, flower petals, or a dotted loading spinner.
Style/medium: premium flat vector-like icon, solid simple geometry and carefully optically balanced negative space.
Color palette: full edge-to-edge deep forest green #185348 background; warm ivory #F4F2E8 beads; restrained copper #A66E4E separator.
Composition: one centered large pictogram, ring slightly left to balance the short lower-right tassel, occupying about 65 percent of square, entirely within the central 72 percent safe area. Full square canvas, straight corners.
Text: absolutely no text, no letters, no name, no numbers.
Constraints: produce ONE standalone icon only. No mockup, grid, phone, ornamental frame, mosque, crescent, star, gradients, 3D, textures, shadows, rounded outer rectangle, glow, watermark, or extra graphics. Distinct silhouette, thick forms and high contrast are essential.
```

## البديل 3

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one square Android app/store icon, optimized for clarity at 32–48 pixels.
Primary request: Create a third distinctly different minimalist icon proposal for an Arabic daily adhkar reader and tasbih app called اناء. Input image is only a brand color palette reference.
Subject: A bold simple OPEN BOOK viewed straight-on from slightly above: two broad symmetrical forest-green page silhouettes, a crisp V-shaped central fold with a wide ivory gap, and subtly curved lower edges. A single SMALL copper bookmark tab is integrated at the top of the center fold, remaining clearly part of the book. The two page forms must read immediately as an open prayer book, with no written text, no fine lines, no extra symbols.
Style/medium: strong flat vector-like geometry, confident solid silhouettes, modern memorable brand icon.
Color palette: full edge-to-edge warm ivory #F4F2E8 background; deep forest green #185348 book; copper #A66E4E bookmark accent.
Composition: one centered large broad book pictogram occupying about 68 percent of width, optically centered, thick filled shapes and generous negative space, all content inside central 72 percent safe area. Full square canvas, straight corners.
Text: absolutely no text, no letters, no app name, no captions.
Constraints: ONE standalone icon only. No mockup, grid, phone, frame, crescent, stars, thin line art, gradients, textures, shadows, 3D, outer rounded rectangle, glow, watermark, or decorative clutter. Simple immediately understandable silhouette at small sizes.
```
