# Leading Android Qibla-direction apps and features (state as of Nov 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-10. Scope: Qibla direction only (prayer times and Hijri calendar are covered elsewhere).
Method note: Google Play pages could not be read (they render by script and returned only the title), and AppBrain/chrome-stats returned HTTP 403. Store figures below come from AppGoblin pages (dated Oct-Nov 2026) and AppBrain search-result snippets. Figures from AppBrain snippets are not page-verified. Vendor pages and "best of 2026" lists are often self-promotional; this is flagged next to each claim.

## 1. Apps covered: rating, installs, pricing, ads

### Takeaway
The biggest Qibla experiences on Android are inside all-in-one prayer apps (Muslim Pro at about 148M installs) and stand-alone ad-heavy compass apps (9D "Qibla Compass" about 36M, Qibla Connect about 28M). Nearly all large ones carry many ad SDKs plus paid tiers. The clean, ad-free option most often recommended is Pillars, and ad-free is itself a selling point in 2026 roundups.

## 2. How the direction is presented

### Takeaway
Four presentation styles exist: (a) flat compass dial with a Kaaba icon or needle (almost every app), (b) AR camera overlay (Google, Muslim Pro, Athan by some accounts, several stand-alone apps), (c) map view with a line toward Makkah (Google desktop, IslamicFinder Athan, Athan Pro, small apps like Qibla Map), and (d) text guidance ("Turn X° right", "You're facing Qibla"). Distance to Makkah and degrees from North are common extras.

### Cited Findings
- **Google Qibla Finder** (launched June 2017, older info but still the reference design): on phones it overlays a blue line on the camera view pointing toward the Kaaba, an arrow guides the user, and an overlay shows distance in km to the Grand Mosque; camera can be turned off and the tool still works; on desktop it shows a simplified Google Maps view with a blue line. — [9to5Google, 2017](https://9to5google.com/2017/06/12/google-qibla-finder-ar-web-app/); [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/article/google-qibla-finder-muslim-pray)
- Google's help page (current): it finds "the great-circle distance" with the haversine formula, Kaaba at Lat 21.4224779, Long 39.8251832, and notes "when viewed on a flat map, the line can sometimes appear bent due to the curvature of the earth." — [Google Help](https://support.google.com/faqs/answer/7364753)
- The site still answers with the title "Qibla Finder - Google" (fetched 2026-11-10; content is script-rendered, so current UI was not seen). — [qiblafinder.withgoogle.com](https://qiblafinder.withgoogle.com/)
- **Muslim Pro**: flat compass plus an AR mode; also shows distance to Makkah and current coordinates. — [Just Pray blog](https://justprayapp.co/blog/best-qibla-finder-app); AR "on supported phones" — [Al Muslim Plus test, Jun 2026, self-promotional](https://app.almuslimplus.com/en/articles/best-qibla-direction-app_616)
- **Athan by IslamicFinder**: compass with a directional arrow, plus a map view showing the line from the user's location to Makkah. — [Just Pray blog](https://justprayapp.co/blog/best-qibla-finder-app)
- **Athan Pro (Quanticapps)**: section "High-Precision Qibla Direction": built-in compass to the Kaaba, "a live map showing the Qibla direction and the shortest path to Mecca", and distance in miles or km. — [AppGoblin listing text](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.quanticapps.athan). Other sources also credit Athan Pro with an AR camera view. — [search result summary of App Store/review pages](https://apps.apple.com/app/id743843090) (not confirmed on the Android listing, which does not mention AR)
- **Qibla Connect**: described as a straightforward compass with a Kaaba icon; no AR or map mode found. — [Al Muslim Plus test](https://app.almuslimplus.com/en/articles/best-qibla-direction-app_616); listing says it "uses the device's compass and GPS" — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.quranreading.qibladirection)
- **Pillars**: compass with N/S/W/E marks pointing to the Kaaba; screenshot shows the text "You're facing Makkah"; manual city selection available. — [Pillars](https://thepillarsapp.com/qibla-finder-app)
- **Qibla Map (Tywo LLC, iOS, 2026)**: map mode with an arrow toward Makkah and address search to see direction from any place; compass mode with "Turn X° right" prompts and "You're facing Qibla" when within 12°; "Precise bearing in degrees from North"; Kaaba coordinates 21.4225 N, 39.8262 E; map works offline after first load. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6760588381)
- Qibla Direction Offline (Android) is listed with an overhead map view and AR. — [Muslim Qibla](https://muslimqibla.com/best-qibla-compass-apps-2026-most-accurate-qibla-finder/)

### Inferences
- For an app without internet, a tile-based map line (Google Maps style) is not possible; the realistic equivalents are a bearing in degrees from true North, the distance in km, and possibly a small offline-drawn globe or world outline with the great-circle line.
- Text guidance ("turn 23° right") plus a clear "facing" state is cheap to build and is what the newest small apps use.

### Gaps
- No primary screenshots of current Muslim Pro, Athan Pro, IslamicFinder, Prayer Now or Al-Moazin Qibla screens were obtainable (store pages unreadable).

## 3. Accuracy handling (declination, calibration, interference, feedback, no magnetometer)

### Takeaway
Real accuracy is limited by the phone's magnetometer (several degrees indoors), not by the math. Good apps (1) correct magnetic to true north, (2) ask for a figure-8 calibration when the sensor reports low accuracy, (3) warn about metal and magnetic cases, and (4) offer a non-magnetic fallback (sun, map). Few apps document any of this; no rigorous independent accuracy test was found.

### Cited Findings
- Android's orientation azimuth is "the angle between the device's current compass direction and magnetic north" — so any app that does not add declination shows a magnetic-north-based Qibla. — [Android Developers: Position sensors](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/sensors/sensors_position)
- `GeomagneticField.getDeclination()` gives declination from true north; the class "currently uses WMM-2020 which is valid until 2025, but should produce acceptable results for several years after that." Inputs: latitude, longitude, altitude, time (approximate is fine). Works fully offline. — [Android Developers: GeomagneticField](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/hardware/GeomagneticField)
- Android docs: "you can improve the sensor's accuracy by waving the device in a figure-8 pattern"; the magnetometer is rate-limited when targeting Android 12+; the game rotation vector "does not use the geomagnetic field" (so it cannot find north); the geomagnetic rotation vector has lower accuracy but lower power; `onAccuracyChanged` must be implemented. — [Android Developers: Position sensors](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/sensors/sensors_position)
- Some App Store Qibla apps state their "directional meter ... is based on magnetic north", i.e. no declination correction. — [search summary of App Store listings](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1562059677)
- Google: "calibrate your compass before using" Qibla Finder; the help page says nothing about declination. — [Google Help](https://support.google.com/faqs/answer/7364753)
- Pillars: accuracy depends on holding the phone flat and away from metal, magnets and magnetic-clasp cases which "can throw a phone compass off"; figure-of-eight if the reading looks wrong. — [Pillars](https://thepillarsapp.com/qibla-finder-app)
- Al Muslim Plus test (Jun 2026, Android 14, London and Istanbul, three repeats, some near a fridge and Wi-Fi router; self-promotional and no raw data): Al Muslim Plus under 2°; IslamicFinder under 2°; Qibla Compass (i-Soft) about 4° indoors / 3° outdoors; Muslim Pro about 3-4°; Qibla Connect within 5° outdoors, "slow to stabilize", needle flickers near electronics. Al Muslim Plus includes calibration reminders. — [Al Muslim Plus](https://app.almuslimplus.com/en/articles/best-qibla-direction-app_616)
- Muslim Qibla comparison claims iQibla "within 0.1 degrees" of geodetic benchmarks and Azzure Labs differs "less than 0.3 degrees"; benchmark, devices and locations not named; only iQibla is stated to correct declination; only Qibla Direction Offline shows a GPS accuracy indicator; table labels apps "ellipsoidal" vs "spherical". — [Muslim Qibla](https://muslimqibla.com/best-qibla-compass-apps-2026-most-accurate-qibla-finder/)
- Qibla Map shows "You're facing Qibla" when within 12° (a wide tolerance). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6760588381)
- Phones without a magnetometer: some Qibla apps simply require a compass sensor; Sun Qibla (iOS, free) uses the Sun's or Moon's position instead of any magnetic sensor ("free from magnetic interference"): point the top of the phone at the object and align the bottom with its shadow; it can also show true north. Sun Compass (Android) targets devices without a magnetic compass. — [Sun Qibla, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sun-qibla/id1478091779); [FileHippo: Sun Compass with Qibla angle](https://filehippo.com/android/download_sun-compass-with-qibla-angle/)

### Inferences
- Spherical vs ellipsoidal Qibla math differ by small fractions of a degree, far below the 2-5° sensor error measured above; marketing "0.1° accuracy" claims describe the math, not what the user sees.
- The most valuable trust features for a small offline app: true-north correction via `GeomagneticField`, a visible accuracy state from `onAccuracyChanged` (with a figure-8 prompt only when accuracy is LOW/UNRELIABLE), a warning about magnetic cases, a sun-based check that needs no magnetometer, and showing the bearing number so users can cross-check with a physical compass or a mosque.
- Since WMM-2020 is past its nominal validity, declination on older Android builds will drift slightly (typically small); an app could bundle its own WMM2025 coefficients if it wants to be strict.

### Gaps
- No source found confirming haptic vibration or colour change behaviour in Muslim Pro, IslamicFinder, Athan Pro, Qibla Connect or Pillars.
- Whether newer Android versions (16/17) ship WMM2025 in `GeomagneticField` was not confirmed; the doc page still says WMM-2020.
- No independent, published-data accuracy test (2023-2026) was found; all tests found are by app vendors.

## 4. Location sources and offline use

### Takeaway
The math needs only latitude and longitude, so offline Qibla is easy; leaders vary: Google needs internet, Pillars and several stand-alone apps work offline once location is known, and manual city selection is the common fallback.

### Cited Findings
- Google Qibla Finder: location "provided by GPS or manually"; GPS and camera (Android) permissions; data "never stored". — [Google Help](https://support.google.com/faqs/answer/7364753). Requires an internet connection. — [Just Pray blog](https://justprayapp.co/blog/best-qibla-finder-app)
- Pillars: direction calculated on the phone, no internet needed once location is set, works abroad without mobile data, location not sent to Pillars; manual city option. — [Pillars](https://thepillarsapp.com/qibla-finder-app)
- IslamicFinder Qibla works offline after initial geolocation; Qibla Compass (i-Soft) works offline using last known GPS position. — [Al Muslim Plus](https://app.almuslimplus.com/en/articles/best-qibla-direction-app_616)
- Muslim Pro and Athan rated "partial" offline; Google "no"; Qibla Direction Offline "full"; Islam.ms stores last location for offline use. — [Muslim Qibla](https://muslimqibla.com/best-qibla-compass-apps-2026-most-accurate-qibla-finder/); [search summary](https://www.brandsynario.com/technology/qibla-finder-apps/)
- Muslim Pro's help page for Qibla starts with enabling location services in device settings. — [Muslim Pro Help](https://support.muslimpro.com/hc/en-us/articles/200664360-How-To-Access-Qibla-on-Muslim-Pro-App)
- Arabic "اتجاه القبله الدقيق" (iOS) works without internet and lets the user pick the current city or allow location. — [App Store SA](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/%D8%A7%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D9%87-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%82%D8%A8%D9%84%D9%87-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D9%82%D9%8A%D9%82/id879837440?l=ar&uo=4)

### Inferences
- For an app with no internet permission: GPS fix (or last known location from the OS) plus a bundled city list for manual choice covers every case; city-level precision is enough because moving tens of km barely changes the bearing except very close to Makkah.

### Gaps
- No source found on whether apps use network (Wi-Fi/cell) location vs GPS specifically.

## 5. Visual design (minimal vs ornate, dark mode, Arabic layout)

### Takeaway
2026 praise goes to clean, calm, minimal screens with plain text confirmation ("You're facing Makkah"); ornate dials and many visual themes are a stand-alone-app pattern tied to ad-funded apps. Hard evidence on dark mode and Arabic layout of each app was not found.

### Cited Findings
- Pillars reviewers describe the interface as "clean, simple, and calming"; no ads "covering the compass". — [Pillars](https://thepillarsapp.com/qibla-finder-app)
- IslamicFinder is criticised for a "dated interface"; Qibla Compass (i-Soft) for small text; Qibla Compass (i-Soft) praised for fast loading and "minimal distraction". — [Al Muslim Plus](https://app.almuslimplus.com/en/articles/best-qibla-direction-app_616)
- Google's design: camera view, one blue line, an arrow, km distance; camera optional. — [9to5Google, 2017](https://9to5google.com/2017/06/12/google-qibla-finder-ar-web-app/)
- Accessibility: an iOS user of alQibla reported the compass screen became harder to use with VoiceOver after an update. — [App Store: alQibla](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id322401779)

### Inferences
- A minimal dial (large, still, high contrast), a Kaaba mark at the Qibla bearing, a single numeric bearing, and a clear "facing" state matches what is praised and avoids what is criticised (clutter, small text, ads).
- Accessibility (TalkBack announcing "turn 20 degrees right") is a gap in the market.

### Gaps
- No reliable source on which leading apps support dark mode or a right-to-left Arabic Qibla layout.

## 8. Qibla on map and sun/shadow Qibla

### Takeaway
Map views with a line to Makkah exist (Google desktop, IslamicFinder, Athan Pro, small apps). The sun method has fixed dates each year when the sun is directly above the Kaaba; in 2026 these were May 27-28 at about 12:18 and July 15-16 at about 12:27 Makkah time. A sun-azimuth method that works any sunny hour exists in Sun Qibla.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 sun-over-Kaaba dates: May 27 or 28 at about 12:18 PM Makkah time; sun altitude 89.89° on May 27 and 89.94° on May 28 (Jeddah Astronomical Society figures as reported); second event July 15 or 16 at about 12:27 PM. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/sun-aligns-directly-over-kaaba-in-rare-celestial-event-offering-muslims-precise-qibla-direction-1.500199170); [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2645289)
- Indonesia's BMKG gave 16:18 WIB (late May) and 16:27 WIB (mid-July) and advised being ready about 5 minutes early; one source gives a 15-17 July window. Method: at that moment vertical objects' shadows point away from the Kaaba, so Qibla is opposite the shadow. — [Qoo10 ID](https://www.qoo10.co.id/en/tech/127316/sun-over-the-kaaba-qibla-direction-check/)
- Saudi National Center for Meteorology clarified the alignment does not cause heatwaves. — [search summary of Gulf press](https://english.aawsat.com/node/5147688)
- Sun Qibla app computes Qibla from the Sun's or Moon's position and the device location, no magnetometer. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sun-qibla/id1478091779)
- iQibla is the only app in one 2026 table with "sun/moon verification". — [Muslim Qibla](https://muslimqibla.com/best-qibla-compass-apps-2026-most-accurate-qibla-finder/)
- Map line examples: Google desktop blue line on Google Maps; IslamicFinder map line; Athan Pro "shortest path to Mecca"; Qibla Map (iOS) arrow on a street map. — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2017/06/12/google-qibla-finder-ar-web-app/); [Just Pray](https://justprayapp.co/blog/best-qibla-finder-app); [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.quanticapps.athan); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6760588381)

### Inferences
- An offline app can compute the sun's azimuth for any time and place with a standard solar-position formula and show "the Qibla is X° to the right of the sun" or "of your shadow": this works on phones with no magnetometer and is immune to metal and magnets. It is a strong, unique offline trust feature.
- The yearly "sun over Kaaba" moments can be computed locally each year (no need to hard-code 2026 dates).

### Gaps
- The two yearly "anti-Qibla" moments (sun directly over the point opposite the Kaaba, which give a second shadow check for the Americas) were not verified with a source in this pass.
- No Android-specific sun-Qibla app with ratings data was found.
